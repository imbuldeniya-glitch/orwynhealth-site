Standards and governance

How Orwyn is held to account.

The principle

Built to the standard before anyone requires it

Clinical software earns trust by being inspectable, and that applies to the company as much as the code. This page sets out the standards Orwyn is being built to, and where each one stands today. Where work is in progress, it says so: formal conformance claims will be made only when the relevant assessment or certification is complete.

The standards

What we build to, and where each one stands

Clinical safety: DCB0129

DCB0129 is the NHS clinical risk management standard for manufacturers of health software. Orwyn's hazard log, risk management plan and clinical safety case are in development, and an independent clinical safety officer will review the safety case before any deployment. We also support deploying organisations with their own duties under the companion standard, DCB0160.

Status: in progress, independent review before any deployment.

Medical device regulation

Orwyn is health technology built to the engineering and documentation standards expected of regulated clinical software under UK medical device regulation, with specialist regulatory advice taken before any classification commitment, and the intention to certify.

Status: built to the standard from the first line of code; classification confirmed with regulatory advice before launch.

NHS Digital Technology Assessment Criteria

The DTAC is the NHS's baseline assessment for digital health technologies, covering clinical safety, data protection, technical security, interoperability and usability. Orwyn is being mapped against every DTAC criterion as it is built, so that assessment is a formality rather than a scramble.

Status: mapping in progress.

Data protection and cyber security

UK GDPR by design, with data minimisation as the starting point rather than an afterthought. This website sets no cookies and runs no analytics or tracking. Cyber Essentials certification is planned ahead of first deployment, alongside the NHS Data Security and Protection Toolkit.

Status: data minimisation live today; certifications scheduled before deployment.

Data minimisation

The least data we can possibly hold

In its first version, Orwyn asks nothing of patients: no app, no login, no password, and nothing collected back from the patient. The handover a patient receives is read-only, handed over by their clinician in the room. For an information governance reviewer, the shortest route through a data protection impact assessment is a product that barely collects data, and that is a design decision, not an accident.
