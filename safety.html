Safety and how it is built

Written rules, not a black box.

Explainable by design

Every decision can be shown and inspected

Orwyn runs on rules a named consultant surgeon has written and signed off, not on a model whose reasoning cannot be inspected. Anyone reviewing a decision can be shown why it was made.

Safety first, deliberately

Safety questions come before anything else

Safety questions must be answered before Orwyn will suggest anything at all. That behaviour is deliberate and it does not bend.

Standards

Built for the standard it will be held to

Orwyn is health technology built to the engineering and safety standards expected of regulated clinical software, with a clinical safety case that will be reviewed by an independent clinical safety officer before any deployment, and the intention to certify.

Knee first, then hip. One joint done properly before the next.

Who wrote the rules?

Orwyn is founded and led by a consultant orthopaedic knee and hip surgeon.

Who is behind Orwyn
Decision support only. The clinician assesses the patient and makes the final decision. Orwyn is not a service for patients and does not provide medical advice.
