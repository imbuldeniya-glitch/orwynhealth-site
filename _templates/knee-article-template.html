What this page does not cover

Urgent and emergency presentations follow local pathways

Serious pathology as a cause of musculoskeletal symptoms is rare, and it is managed as an emergency or an urgent onward referral under local pathways rather than through advice or decision support. See the ARMA guidance on urgent and emergency musculoskeletal conditions.
