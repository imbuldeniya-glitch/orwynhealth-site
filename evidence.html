Evidence

What we have tested, and what the first trial will measure.

Orwyn has no results to publish yet, and will not claim any until an evaluation is complete and has been reviewed. This page says exactly what exists today, what has been tested and how, and what the first trial will measure. Where work is unfinished, it says so.

What has been tested, and how

The software is tested automatically every time it changes, so that a change in one place cannot quietly alter an answer somewhere else. The clinical rules are written by the surgeon who governs them, each linked to the evidence behind it, and the safety rules have been approved for evaluation by the Clinical Safety Officer. Orwyn has not yet been used with real patients.

What the first trial will measure

  • Process. Whether Orwyn fits inside a normal appointment, and where it does not.
  • Decisions, in both directions. Patients referred who did not need a surgical opinion, and patients who needed one sooner.
  • The pathway. Time to the right next step, re-attendance, imaging and escalation.
  • User experience. What clinicians and patients say about the plan, the letters and the time taken.
  • Safety. Every safety rule that applied, what was done, and any incident.
