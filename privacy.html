Privacy

What this website does with your information.

This website sets no cookies, runs no analytics and no advertising or tracking scripts, and collects nothing about you as you read it. Web fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which will see your device's internet address when the page loads.

If you email us, we hold your message and your email address so we can reply, and for as long as the conversation is useful. We do not add you to any mailing list and we do not pass your details to anyone else. Ask us to delete them at any time and we will.

Please do not send patient-identifiable or confidential clinical information to this email address.

Orwynhealth Ltd is the data controller. Write to us at the registered office address in the footer, or to hello@orwynhealth.com. You can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Decision support for clinicians only. The clinician assesses the patient and makes every decision. Orwyn is not a service for patients and gives no advice directly to patients or the public.
