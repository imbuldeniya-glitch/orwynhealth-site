Reference pages

MSK pathway resources.

Why these exist

Plain explanations of the things everyone uses and nobody defines

Musculoskeletal care runs on terms that mean different things in different services. Triage, first contact, advanced practice, referral optimisation. These pages set out what each one actually means, who does what, and where the decisions are made.

They are written for clinicians working in or alongside these services, for medical and surgical colleagues who were never taught how the front of the pathway works, and for the service leads and commissioners who design and fund it. Every substantive claim is linked to its source. They are written by Dr Arj Imbuldeniya, consultant orthopaedic knee and hip surgeon, and corrected when people who do this work tell us we have it wrong.
