Working with the NHS

Developed with the people who do the work.

Who it is for

Clinicians, trusts and the services that commission care

Orwyn is being co-designed with the clinicians who will use it and with patients, and is designed for NHS Trusts and the services that commission musculoskeletal care.

Patient involvement sits at the centre of that work, because the best-designed pathway still fails if patients do not believe the plan, understand it, or cannot realistically follow it.

For the services that pay for musculoskeletal care the point is practical: fewer referrals that add nothing, earlier surgical review for the patients who need it, and a recorded reason for every decision.

If you run or commission a musculoskeletal service, or you see these patients first and want to say what would actually help, we would like to hear from you.

If you want the plain version of how these roles fit together, we have written one: first contact practitioners and MSK roles, explained.
