Who is behind it

A surgeon who watched the same thing happen for ten years.

The founder

Dr Arj Imbuldeniya

Orwyn is founded and led by Dr Arj Imbuldeniya, a consultant orthopaedic knee and hip surgeon. He has been an NHS consultant at Chelsea and Westminster Hospital NHS Foundation Trust in London for over a decade, and his practice spans the full range of modern orthopaedics for the knee and hip, from biological and regenerative treatment through to robotic-assisted joint replacement.

The idea came from more than ten years of watching the same thing happen. People wait too long to see the right person, and the decision that shapes everything afterwards gets made without the surgical input it deserves. Orwyn is an attempt to put that input where the decision is actually made, rather than months later.

Orwyn is being built through the NHS Clinical Entrepreneur Programme (Cohort 10), the NHS's own programme for clinicians turning frontline experience into NHS innovation. You can find Dr Imbuldeniya on LinkedIn.

Orwyn is an independent company. It is not a Chelsea and Westminster Hospital NHS Foundation Trust product, and no NHS organisation endorses it. Dr Imbuldeniya's NHS role is given here as background only.

Working with the NHS

Orwyn is being developed with NHS musculoskeletal clinicians, and is designed for NHS trusts and the services that commission musculoskeletal care.

How Orwyn works with the NHS
Decision support only. The clinician assesses the patient and makes the final decision. Orwyn is not a service for patients and does not provide medical advice.
