The problem

Musculoskeletal care goes wrong in both directions

Some patients are sent for a surgical opinion they do not need, and wait months to be told so. Others are managed for a year with a problem that needed an operation. Both happen for the same reason: the person seeing the patient first has no way to reach the judgement of the person at the end of the pathway.

Musculoskeletal means the joints, muscles, bones and soft tissues. First-contact practitioners are the physiotherapists and clinicians who see these patients before anyone else, usually in general practice or a community service. They are experienced and they are not the problem. The problem is that the system gives them no way to reach a surgical opinion at the moment the decision is actually made.

What Orwyn is

A surgeon's criteria, written down and shown

Most patients do not need it. An experienced clinician is certain about most of the people they see, and existing triage stays exactly where it is. Orwyn is for the referral decisions where the surgical question is genuinely in play, and for making the reasoning visible when it is.

It is used by the clinician

Orwyn is not a patient app and not a questionnaire the patient fills in at home. It sits with the clinician while the patient is in front of them, and it fits inside a normal appointment.

It shows its reasons

Every routing suggestion comes with the reason for it, in plain words. There are no scores and no percentages. The clinician can disagree and record why, and that disagreement is kept.

It works with you, not above you

Orwyn does not check, approve or oversee the clinician's work. It carries a surgeon's judgement into a room the surgeon is not in. The clinician assesses the patient and decides.

Where it comes from

Built with clinicians, from evidence and experience

Orwyn was not designed at a desk. It draws on decades of seeing and treating thousands of patients, surgically and non surgically, and on conversations about this problem with well over a hundred clinicians: physiotherapists, advanced practice physiotherapists, first-contact practitioners and orthopaedic surgeons. The consensus, wherever the question was asked, was the same. Better decisions for patients are made together.

The routing criteria are built from the highest quality evidence available in published research and national guidance, combined with that clinical experience. When a consultant surgeon and a physiotherapist work side by side, patients reach the right decision sooner. Orwyn exists to make that working relationship available in every clinic, at a cost the NHS can afford.

How it is built

Written rules, not a black box

Explainable by design

Orwyn runs on rules a named consultant surgeon has written and signed off, not on a model whose reasoning cannot be inspected. Anyone reviewing a decision can be shown why it was made.

Safety questions must be answered before Orwyn will suggest anything at all. That behaviour is deliberate and it does not bend.

Built for the standard it will be held to

Orwyn is health technology built to the engineering and safety standards expected of regulated clinical software, with a clinical safety case that will be reviewed by an independent clinical safety officer before any deployment, and the intention to certify.

Who is behind it

Dr Arj Imbuldeniya

Orwyn is founded and led by Dr Arj Imbuldeniya, a consultant orthopaedic knee and hip surgeon. He has been an NHS consultant at Chelsea and Westminster Hospital NHS Foundation Trust in London for over a decade, and his practice spans the full range of modern orthopaedics for the knee and hip, from biological and regenerative treatment through to robotic-assisted joint replacement.

The idea came from more than ten years of watching the same thing happen. People wait too long to see the right person, and the decision that shapes everything afterwards gets made without the surgical input it deserves. Orwyn is an attempt to put that input where the decision is actually made, rather than months later.

Orwyn is being built through the NHS Clinical Entrepreneur Programme (Cohort 10), the NHS's own programme for clinicians turning frontline experience into NHS innovation. You can find Dr Imbuldeniya on LinkedIn.

Orwyn is an independent company. It is not a Chelsea and Westminster Hospital NHS Foundation Trust product, and no NHS organisation endorses it. Dr Imbuldeniya's NHS role is given here as background only.

Who it is for

Working with the NHS

Orwyn is being developed with NHS musculoskeletal clinicians, and is designed for NHS trusts and the services that commission musculoskeletal care.

For the services that pay for musculoskeletal care the point is practical: fewer referrals that add nothing, earlier surgical review for the patients who need it, and a recorded reason for every routing decision.

If you run or commission a musculoskeletal service, or you see these patients first and want to say what would actually help, we would like to hear from you.
