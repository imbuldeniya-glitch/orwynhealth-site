The problem

Musculoskeletal care goes wrong in both directions

Some patients are sent for a surgical opinion they do not need, and wait months to be told so. Others are managed for a year with a problem that needed an operation. Both happen for the same reason: the person seeing the patient first has no way to reach the judgement of the person at the end of the pathway.

What Orwyn is

A surgeon's criteria, written down and shown

Every suggestion Orwyn makes comes with the reason for it, in plain words. There are no scores and no percentages. The clinician can disagree and record why, and that disagreement is kept.

Used by the clinician

Orwyn is not a patient app and not a questionnaire the patient fills in at home. It sits with the clinician while the patient is in front of them, and it fits inside a normal appointment.

It shows its reasons

Orwyn runs on rules a named consultant surgeon has written and signed off, built from the strongest available research evidence and national guidance: NICE, the Getting It Right First Time programme and the British orthopaedic specialist societies. It is not a model whose reasoning cannot be inspected. Anyone reviewing a decision can be shown why it was made.

With you, not above you

Orwyn does not check, approve or oversee the clinician's work. It carries a surgeon's judgement into a room the surgeon is not in. The clinician assesses the patient and decides.
